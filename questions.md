# Exam-Ready Check: Question Set v1

15 questions: 5 per section, each with 1 easy (typed), 2 medium (typed) and 2 hard (choose, with "I'm not sure"). Instructions are in English, questions in German. No answer is a number. Topic tags stay hidden during the test and are used only for the practice tips.

## Vocabulary

Instruction for typed questions: Type the German word for the English word in brackets.

| # | Level | Question | Correct answer | Tag |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | Easy, typed | Ich wohne in einer \_\_\_. (flat) | Wohnung | Home |
| V2 | Medium, typed | Ich suche ein \_\_\_ für zwei Nächte. (room) | Zimmer | Hotel and travel |
| V3 | Medium, typed | Die Jacke ist schön, aber sie ist zu \_\_\_. (expensive) | teuer | Shopping |
| V4 | Hard, choose | You are in a restaurant and want to pay. Choose the German word for "the bill". Options: die Rechnung / die Karte / die Kasse / I'm not sure | die Rechnung | Restaurant |
| V5 | Hard, choose | Choose the German word for "yesterday". Options: gestern / morgen / heute / I'm not sure | gestern | Time words |

## Cross-check notes

- Every answer was checked as standard A1 German. None of the answers is a number.
- All 15 are new, none taken from the design doc samples. No answer is a cognate or loanword (like Pizza or Pasta), so it cannot be guessed from English.
- Büro and Kino have fewer than 5 letters, so the one-wrong-letter rule does not apply to them. "Buero" is still accepted under the umlaut rule.
- In R2 and R3 the text includes a wrong option ("kaputt" door, "Park"), so copying any word from the text does not work.
